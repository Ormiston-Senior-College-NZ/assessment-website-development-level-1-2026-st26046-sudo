Who Can Join?

Any student at Ormiston Senior College can join the Club of Indian Diaspora. You do not have to be Indian to participate. Everyone interested in learning about Indian culture is welcome.

Leadership Opportunities

Students can become involved in leadership by helping organise cultural events, managing activities, and representing the club within the school. This helps students develop valuable skills while promoting cultural awareness.

Teacher in Charge

The club is supported by teachers at Ormiston Senior College who help guide students and organise club events. Their guidance ensures that activities are educational, fun, and safe for everyone involved.
