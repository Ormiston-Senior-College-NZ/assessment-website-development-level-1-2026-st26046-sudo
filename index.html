Welcome to the Club of Indian Diaspora

The Club of Indian Diaspora (CID) is a student club at Ormiston Senior College that celebrates Indian culture, traditions and heritage. Our club provides a welcoming environment where students can learn about Indian festivals, music, dance and food while building friendships with others.

Students from all cultures are welcome to join the club and participate in activities that promote cultural understanding and diversity within the school community. The club allows students to express their identity and learn more about the rich traditions that come from different parts of India.

Our Vision

Our vision is to create a strong cultural community within the school where students can celebrate Indian traditions, learn about different cultures, and share their experiences with others. Through events and activities we aim to promote unity, respect and cultural pride.

What We Do

Throughout the year the Club of Indian Diaspora organises different cultural activities such as celebrating festivals like Diwali and Holi, sharing traditional Indian food, performing cultural dances, and teaching others about the history and traditions of India. These activities help students feel connected to their culture while also educating others in the school community.
