Club Activities

The Club of Indian Diaspora organises a variety of cultural activities throughout the year to help students experience and appreciate Indian culture. Students get the chance to perform, learn, and share cultural traditions with others in the school community.

  • Cultural performances showcasing dance and theatre
  • Indian music workshops
  • Traditional food sharing and cooking events
  • Cultural presentations and storytelling
  • Community service events and outreach programs

Festivals We Celebrate

Our club celebrates a wide range of Indian festivals to give students an authentic cultural experience:

  • Holi – The festival of colours celebrating joy and unity.
  • Baisakhi – A harvest festival celebrated in Punjab.
  • Diwali – The festival of lights symbolising victory of light over darkness.
  • Indian Diaspora Week – Celebrating Indian heritage worldwide.
  • Indian Independence Day – Remembering India’s journey to freedom.
